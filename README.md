# 🛒 Blinkit Data Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Blinkit's sales, customer, delivery, inventory, and marketing data to identify key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, operational bottlenecks, and campaign performance.

The objective is to generate actionable insights and recommendations that support data-driven business decisions, improve operational efficiency, enhance customer satisfaction, and maximize marketing effectiveness.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

- Identify key revenue drivers across products and categories.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and segment performance.
- Evaluate delivery efficiency and identify operational bottlenecks.
- Measure marketing campaign effectiveness using ROAS and conversion metrics.
- Generate actionable insights and recommendations to support business growth.

## 📈 Key Findings

### Sales Insights

- Total Revenue Generated: ₹11.01M
- Best Revenue Month: May (₹1.18M)
- Top Revenue Category: Dairy & Breakfast (₹0.64M)
- Top Selling Product: Vitamins (₹0.26M)
- Online Payment is the most preferred payment method.

### Customer & Operations Insights

- Regular customers contribute the highest revenue share.
- 69% of orders were delivered on time.
- Average delivery time is 4.44 minutes.
- Delivery delays are concentrated in specific areas.
- Customer feedback is largely positive and neutral.

### Marketing Insights

- Marketing campaigns generated ₹32.19M revenue.
- Overall ROAS achieved is 1.97.
- Referral Program achieved the highest ROAS (2.03).
- Email Channel generated the highest conversion share.
- App and Email channels drive maximum engagement.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### Sales & Inventory

- Increase inventory availability for top-selling products.
- Promote high-performing categories.
- Bundle frequently purchased products.
- Focus on high-margin products.

### Customer & Operations

- Improve delivery performance in delay-prone areas.
- Optimize rider allocation and delivery routes.
- Strengthen customer retention initiatives.
- Continuously monitor customer feedback.

### Marketing

- Increase investment in high-ROAS campaigns.
- Expand Email Marketing initiatives.
- Personalize promotional campaigns.
- Optimize underperforming channels.

## 🏁 Executive Conclusion

This analysis reveals that Blinkit generated ₹11.01M in revenue from 5K orders, with Dairy & Breakfast emerging as the highest revenue-generating category and Vitamins as the top-selling product.

Marketing campaigns generated ₹32.19M in revenue, while the Referral Program achieved the highest ROAS of 2.03. Customer feedback remained largely positive, though delivery delays were concentrated in specific areas, highlighting opportunities for operational improvement.

Overall, the project demonstrates how data analytics can support revenue growth, operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and marketing effectiveness.
